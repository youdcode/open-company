# Company profile

FICTIONAL DEMO COMPANY. Every name, company and link in demo mode is invented.

## What we sell
Online booking for independent physiotherapy clinics: a booking page, automatic reminders, a waitlist
that fills cancelled slots, and an import of the existing patient list. 79 GBP per clinic per month.

## Who buys it
Independent physiotherapy clinics in the UK, 3 to 50 people. Buyer: the owner or the practice manager.

## The problem we solve
Bookings by phone take hours of front-desk time, and no-shows leave gaps in the diary.
